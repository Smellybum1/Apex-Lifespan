# Claim Packet Review

## Goal

Add an explicit audited path for human review of complete existing claim packets whose curated references are not source-candidate rows.

## Guardrails

- Do not expose public writes.
- Require an active operator actor, an explicit write gate, and a human review note.
- Refuse incomplete packets.
- Preserve citation traceability and uncertainty labels.
- Do not use this path for automatic source-candidate promotion.

## Validation

- Targeted operator claim-packet tests.
- Focused coverage and launch readiness summaries after any reviewed packet.
